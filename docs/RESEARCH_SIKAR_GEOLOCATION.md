# Sikar Cigar Lounge geolocation

## Research note

Miranda Devine's August 27, 2026 X post reproduces a summary of the public
custody and trial record. It states that FBI investigators geolocated Hunter
Biden's cellphone to the Sikar Cigar Lounge in Wilmington on **April 12,
2019**, shortly before he dropped the laptops at The Mac Shop at approximately
6:50 p.m. The summary says prosecutors used financial-transaction history and
location logs to corroborate John Paul Mac Isaac's account of the repair-shop
drop-off.

The same post states that Hunter's signature appears on repair Quote #7469,
for $85 and an attempted recovery of data from three MacBook Pros, and that the
FBI seized the laptop and a hard drive on December 9, 2019 after Mac Isaac
contacted them. Those points are consistent with the court-record chronology
already documented in this project, but the image itself is a secondary
summary, not the underlying DOJ filing, location-log exhibit, or trial
transcript.

A second image in the same post states that FBI CART verified the laptop's
authenticity and ownership, assessed that it was not manipulated, and deemed it
fit for use as evidence, attributing the account to IRS Agent Gary Shapley's
2023 House Ways and Means Committee testimony. It also states that prosecutor
Derek Hines displayed the silver MacBook Pro to the jury in Wilmington on June
4, 2024, before FBI Agent Erika Jensen identified it by its serial number.
These assertions require citation to the underlying testimony, filings, and
trial transcript before being treated as independently verified findings here.
