- Delaware Supreme Court, *John Paul Mac Isaac v. Politico LLC*, No. 448, 2024 (25 Aug 2025). Repair sequence, FBI notification, 9 Dec 2019 subpoena, exact copy before surrender, Costello/Giuliani/NY Post chain; dissent’s three-laptop/keyboard detail. https://law.justia.com/cases/delaware/supreme-court/2025/448-2024.html
- Delaware Superior Court, *Mac Isaac v. Cable News Network, Inc.*, C.A. No. S22C-10-012 RHR (30 Sep 2024). Repair authorization, 13 Apr drive, invoice, FBI contacts, subpoena, retained copy, Aug 2020 Costello transfer. https://law.justia.com/cases/delaware/superior-court/2024/s22c-10-012-rhr.html
