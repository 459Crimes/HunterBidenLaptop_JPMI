## Findings

Apelbaum’s account on this call:

1. **Third week of October 2020**, about **two hours** after he says he gave the **FBI** a “complete dump on the laptop,” he met **Tucker Carlson’s executive producer** in a hotel room for about **four hours**.
2. He says the producer had **full access to the laptop**, including an **FBI report**, verified the material, and received a **USB drive**.
3. He says the producer spoke to Carlson and they intended to publish, about **a week before the election**.
4. He identifies Carlson’s public **UPS-lost-shipment** story as **that USB**: producer in **New York**, Carlson in **California interviewing Tony Bobulinski**, shipment of the drive claimed lost.
5. He says the producer was **not Neil Patel**; he “met with another guy.”
6. He offers a political interpretation that Carlson “handed Biden the election” / “spiked the story,” including an **Ashley Madison** claim. That is Apelbaum’s opinion on the call. It is **not** a JPMI timestamp or hash finding.

**What the recording does not contain:** no discussion of child-sexual-abuse material, “abuse of minors,” or “inappropriate” files as the payload. The object Apelbaum describes handing over is the **laptop dump** he already associated with the Bureau — in this encyclopedia, the **Mac Isaac / JPMI** copy, not 0728.

**Relation to Carlson’s August 2026 interview:** Carlson later said he sent a producer to meet a man “connected to Netanyahu” who pushed an **abuse-of-minors allegation**, and that he did not air that on Fox ([Apelbaum, Fox, Tucker](APELBAUM_FOX.md)). This 2025 call is Apelbaum describing a **hotel-room USB handoff** of the laptop to Carlson’s EP and tying it to the **UPS** episode. He does **not** name himself as a Netanyahu emissary, and he does **not** pitch CSAM on this tape.
