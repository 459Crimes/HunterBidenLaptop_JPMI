# Yaacov Apelbaum

> **Hatnote.** XRVision founder who handled **Mac Isaac’s retained copy** in October 2020 and published image-authentication of NY Post stills. He is **not** the JPMI acquisition custodian (that is Sanders / Della Rocca) and **not** CBS’s Computer Forensics Services. See [People](PEOPLE.md), [Copy lineages](COPY_LINEAGES.md).

## Who he is

**Yaacov Apelbaum** is founder/CEO (also described as CTO) of **XRVision**, an AI / facial-recognition / video-analytics firm. Public profiles: Israeli-born, U.S. Social Security issued Massachusetts 1988; XRVision founded in Singapore 2015 with co-founder Guy Ron; New York–based in later filings ([AZ Central, 16 Nov 2023](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/local/arizona-investigations/2023/11/16/xrvision-owner-yaacov-apelbaum-exposes-voting-machine-breach-plots/71583389007/)). He blogs at [yaacovapelbaum.com](https://www.yaacovapelbaum.com/) (*The Illustrated Primer*).

Do not confuse him with **Jacob Appelbaum** (Tor / security researcher).

## Relation to JPMI integrity

CBS/CFS examined a Della Rocca–supplied **FBI-lineage “exact copy”** and reported no post-April 2019 user files ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/hunter-biden-laptop-data-analysis/)). The [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2022/03/30/hunter-biden-laptop-data-examined/) separately found **other** circulating clones with later writes. Apelbaum’s October 2020 imaging session, if it involved a writable mount of Mac Isaac’s working copy, is exactly the kind of **later handling** this project distinguishes from Hunter-era authorship — and is **not** independently reconstructed from JPMI tables.
