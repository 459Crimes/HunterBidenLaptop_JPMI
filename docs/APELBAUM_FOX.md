# Apelbaum, Fox News, and Tucker Carlson

> **Hatnote.** Yaacov Apelbaum’s attempt to get a **Mac Isaac direct copy** to Fox News / Tucker Carlson. The object is **JPMI-lineage**, not Extra Found Files / 0728. Full speaker-labeled transcript: [11 Mar 2025 call](APELBAUM_TUCKER_2025-03-11.md). Overview of other Fox paths: [Fox / Tucker](FOX_TUCKER.md). Biography: [Apelbaum](APELBAUM.md).

## What was being delivered

The drive Apelbaum was trying to get to Fox News, and specifically to **Tucker Carlson**, was a **John Paul Mac Isaac copy** — the same **direct-copy lineage** this encyclopedia calls **JPMI**.

That copy is **laptop-derived**. Hacking is **not** attributed to JPMI. Extra Found Files / **0728** (unknown origin; the author’s later FBI referral treats 0728 as potentially hacked) is a **different object**. Apelbaum’s Fox/Tucker attempt is **not** a delivery of 0728.

Mac Isaac’s *American Injustice*, as quoted in Hunter Biden’s Delaware Supreme Court brief, places Apelbaum at Mac Isaac’s **Delaware home in October 2020**, attempting a **forensic image** of the copy Mac Isaac still held and **retaining a copy** ([brief](https://courts.delaware.gov/supreme/oralarguments/download.aspx?id=5406), A096 ¶19). Della Rocca later said Apelbaum was asked to **analyze**, not copy ([NY Post, 6 Feb 2023](https://nypost.com/2023/02/06/john-paul-mac-isaac-asks-for-hunter-biden-probe-over-false-laptop-report/)). Both statements are recorded. Neither converts the object into 0728.

The Apelbaum-held disk is **not** hash-identified with the later Crucial X6 / `HB-IMAGE-2022-04-29.E01`. It is the **same lineage**, not the same published image hash.

### What Apelbaum says happened (October 2020)

In the **third week of October**, about **two hours** after he says he gave the **FBI** a “complete dump on the laptop” (Russian and Chinese connections included), he met **Carlson’s executive producer** in a hotel room for about **four hours**:

> “I had a meeting in a hotel room with Tucker’s executive producer. We spent about four hours in a little sleazy hotel room. And he had full access to the laptop, including the FBI report. And we went over the information, and he had the chance to verify everything, and I gave him a USB drive. And he spoke to Tucker, and they were going to publish the information. We’re talking about a week before the elections…” ([transcript 00:00:00–00:00:52](APELBAUM_TUCKER_2025-03-11.md))

He then identifies Carlson’s public **UPS-lost-shipment** story as **that USB**. He says the producer was in **New York** and Carlson was in **California interviewing Tony Bobulinski**:

> “No, Tucker lost it. You don’t remember the story about UPS losing his shipment? … He claims that the shipment from his executive producer from New York… Because he was in California at the time interviewing [Tony] Bobulinski. So he claims that the shipment of the drive was lost, and that’s why he couldn’t do anything about it.” ([00:00:58–00:01:30](APELBAUM_TUCKER_2025-03-11.md))

Carlson did tell viewers in late October 2020 that a producer-mailed flash drive of “Hunter Biden docs” had gone missing at UPS ([NY Mag / Intelligencer](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/2020/10/why-did-tucker-carlson-send-a-flash-drive-through-the-mail.html)). That on-air segment did **not** name Apelbaum. The 2025 call is Apelbaum attaching **his** USB to that episode.

He says the producer he met was **not Neil Patel**: “I didn’t meet with Neil. I met with another guy.” ([00:01:48](APELBAUM_TUCKER_2025-03-11.md))

He also offers a political reading — that Carlson “handed Biden the election,” “spiked the story,” and had previously spiked an **Ashley Madison** story. That is **Apelbaum’s opinion on the call**, not a JPMI timestamp or hash finding.

### What the recording does not contain

The tape has **no** discussion of child-sexual-abuse material, “abuse of minors,” or “inappropriate” files as the payload. The object Apelbaum describes is the **laptop dump** he associated with the Bureau — here, the **Mac Isaac / JPMI** copy, not 0728.

## Carlson’s August 2026 interview with Hunter Biden

The later **Tucker Carlson Show** interview with Hunter Biden, released **10 August 2026**, is a **different** source ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-interview-key-takeaways-12304361); [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-laptop-b3030658.html); [Singju transcript](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/)).

Carlson said the laptop was **real**, but that how the story entered American media was not:

> “The laptop is real. I thought that from day one. But the way that it entered the bloodstream of the American media was not real.” ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-interview-key-takeaways-12304361))

On **Israeli** approaches and **abuse-of-minors** *allegations*:

> “The allegation that there was abuse of minors on the laptop — that came from the Israelis. I’m just telling you that. They came to me with that, so I know that. … it was the Israelis who were pushing that. Fact. Literally to me.” ([Singju](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/); [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-laptop-b3030658.html))

> “They called me. I sent one of my producers out to go meet with one guy connected to Netanyahu. They were behind that. … I didn’t say that on Fox News. I’ll admit it. … I was part of the problem also, but I knew it.” ([Singju](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/); [al.com](https://www.al.com/politics/2026/08/tucker-carlson-claims-he-omitted-key-part-of-hunter-biden-laptop-story-i-was-part-of-the-problem.html))

[The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-laptop-b3030658.html) glossed Carlson as saying Israeli figures **falsely** told him the laptop contained graphic images of children. That is the Independent’s summary, not a JPMI census.

Apelbaum is Israeli-born and, on the 2025 tape, describes getting a **JPMI-lineage** USB to Carlson’s producer. Carlson, in 2026, describes an unnamed Netanyahu-connected visitor and a **CSAM allegation**. This encyclopedia records both. It does **not** treat them as proved to be the same meeting. Apelbaum does **not**, on the 2025 tape, name himself as a Netanyahu emissary or pitch abuse-of-minors files.

## What this does not change about JPMI
