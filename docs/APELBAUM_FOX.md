# Apelbaum, Fox News, and Tucker Carlson (2020 attempt; 2025 recording)

> **Hatnote.** **Yaacov Apelbaum’s attempt to get a Mac Isaac direct copy to Fox News / Tucker Carlson**. The object is **JPMI-lineage**, not Extra Found Files / 0728. Audio and transcript: [11 Mar 2025 call](APELBAUM_TUCKER_2025-03-11.md). Related: [Fox / Tucker](FOX_TUCKER.md) · [Apelbaum](APELBAUM.md).

## What was being delivered

**459Crimes identification:** the drive Apelbaum was trying to get to Fox News, and specifically to **Tucker Carlson**, was a **John Paul Mac Isaac copy** — the same **direct-copy lineage** this encyclopedia calls **JPMI**.

That copy is **laptop-derived**. Hacking is **not** attributed to JPMI. The 0728 Extra Found Files collection (unknown origin; author’s later FBI referral treats 0728 as potentially hacked) is a **different object**. Apelbaum’s Fox/Tucker attempt is **not** a delivery of 0728.

Mac Isaac’s *American Injustice*, as quoted in Hunter Biden’s Delaware Supreme Court brief, already places Apelbaum at Mac Isaac’s **Delaware home in October 2020**, attempting a **forensic image** of the copy Mac Isaac still held and **retaining a copy** ([brief](https://courts.delaware.gov/supreme/oralarguments/download.aspx?id=5406), A096 ¶19). Della Rocca later said Apelbaum was asked to **analyze**, not copy ([NY Post, 6 Feb 2023](https://nypost.com/2023/02/06/john-paul-mac-isaac-asks-for-hunter-biden-probe-over-false-laptop-report/)). Both statements are recorded. Neither converts the object into 0728.

The Apelbaum-held disk is **not** hash-identified with the later Crucial X6 / `HB-IMAGE-2022-04-29.E01` in this repo. It is the **same lineage** (Mac Isaac retained copy), not the same published image hash.

## Carlson’s August 2026 interview with Hunter Biden

The latest Carlson/Hunter Biden conversation on this subject is the **Tucker Carlson Show** interview released **10 August 2026** ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-interview-key-takeaways-12304361); [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-laptop-b3030658.html); [WJLA](https://wjla.com/news/nation-world/tucker-carlson-discusses-insider-trading-israel-ai-with-hunter-biden-on-podcast-candace-owens-nick-fuentes-laptop)). A third-party transcript is at [The Singju Post](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/).

Carlson said the **laptop contents he believed real**, but that **how the story entered American media** was not:

> “The laptop is real. I thought that from day one. But the way that it entered the bloodstream of the American media was not real.” ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-interview-key-takeaways-12304361); [Singju transcript](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/))

On **Israeli** approaches and **abuse-of-minors** allegations, the interview transcript records:

> “The allegation that there was abuse of minors on the laptop — that came from the Israelis. I’m just telling you that. They came to me with that, so I know that. And one of the things that enraged me about the statement from those sleazy intel guys is that they blamed the Russians for it. Now, maybe the Russians had a hand in it, but it was the Israelis who were pushing that. Fact. Literally to me.” ([Singju transcript](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/); [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-laptop-b3030658.html); [al.com](https://www.al.com/politics/2026/08/tucker-carlson-claims-he-omitted-key-part-of-hunter-biden-laptop-story-i-was-part-of-the-problem.html))

> “Because they called me. I sent one of my producers out to go meet with one guy connected to Netanyahu. They were behind that. … And by the way, I didn’t say that on Fox News. I’ll admit it. I didn’t. I was part of the problem also, but I knew it.” ([Singju transcript](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/); [al.com](https://www.al.com/politics/2026/08/tucker-carlson-claims-he-omitted-key-part-of-hunter-biden-laptop-story-i-was-part-of-the-problem.html))

He later referred to “the child molestation thing or whatever” as the allegation being pushed ([Singju transcript](https://singjupost.com/transcript-of-hunter-biden-interview-on-tucker-carlson-show/)).

**What Carlson claimed, and what he did not:**

| Claim in the 2026 interview | Status |
|---|---|
| A producer was sent to meet **a man connected to Benjamin Netanyahu** | Carlson’s account; he gave **no name** |
| **Israelis** pushed an allegation of **abuse of minors** on the laptop | Carlson’s account; he presents the allegation as what *they* said, not as a finding that such files existed |
| He **did not** air that Israeli-source claim on Fox | His own admission |
| The laptop material was **real** | His belief; not a JPMI table |
| Named **Yaacov Apelbaum** as that visitor | **Not** in the published interview text used here |
| Identified the disk as **0728 Extra Found Files** | **Not** claimed |

[The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/tucker-carlson-hunter-biden-laptop-b3030658.html) summarized Carlson as saying Israeli figures **falsely** told him the laptop contained graphic images of children, and that while the laptop had adult material, “there were no explicit images or videos of children.” That is the Independent’s gloss of the interview, not a JPMI census.

This encyclopedia **does not** silently identify Carlson’s unnamed “guy connected to Netanyahu” as Apelbaum. Apelbaum is Israeli-born and did try to get a Mac Isaac copy to Fox/Tucker; those are separate documented facts. Whether Carlson’s unnamed 2020 producer meeting was Apelbaum is **unproved** from the interview text alone. The **11 Mar 2025** recording is the Apelbaum-side primary for the Fox/Tucker attempt.
