# Scope (what this encyclopedia is not)

> **Hatnote.** Boundary page. Main article: [README](../README.md).

## In scope

One lineage: data recovered at **The Mac Shop** in April 2019, preserved in **Mac Isaac direct copies**, later represented by the **Crucial X6 / `HB-IMAGE-2022-04-29.E01` reports** published here as **JPMI**.

Questions this repo is built to answer:

- What storage object was examined?
- What GPT/HFS+ structure does it have?
- What user/application populations does it preserve?
- Which dates are user-era vs copy-creation vs examiner-era vs report-lineage?
- What integrity conclusion does *this* reporting support?
- Which links are still missing?

## Disambiguation: “the Hunter Biden laptop”

That phrase is used in public for, at least:

1. the **physical Mac(s)** at the shop;
2. the **customer external drive**;
3. **Mac Isaac preservation copies** (JPMI’s family);
4. **FBI-held** originals;
5. **Costello / Giuliani / press** extracts;
6. later **mixed internet archives**;
7. the **TRIMARCO group** (TRIMARCO → APFS volume **12 Dec 2020**; CCC **5 Jan 2021**; then MARYMAN / GUSTAV) and the **Todd-altered** fan-out (TODD → HAYES → MPOLO and APFS*) — Costello-line **bootable** siblings, not clones of examined JPMI `Untitled` ([BRANCH_DEVIATIONS](BRANCH_DEVIATIONS.md));
8. **BLAP01 / GAI** — the other COSTELLO grandchild (GAI `Biden Lap 2`, **17 May 2021**);
9. **IPHONE** — iPhone backup on the named copies (password **2021-06-02**);
10. **0728 Extra Found Files** — related to laptop material but **not from the laptop files per se**; many unknown origin; some completely unknown to the laptop.

This encyclopedia is about **(3) as instantiated in the reports of (the X6 / E01)**. Mixing (6), **(7)**, **(8)**, or **(10)** into a sentence about (3) as if they were the same object is a category error. **Marco Polo** used **MPOLO** (Hayes **bootable laptop with user files**, Jun 2021 — a **degraded** copy) plus **(10)**; it **did not analyze JPMI**. Hacking is **not** attributed to (1)–(5) or to JPMI. Files of unknown origin relative to the laptop live in **(10)**. [Integrity](INTEGRITY.md) · [Marco Polo v4](MARCO_POLO.md).
