## John Paul Mac Isaac

Proprietor of **The Mac Shop, Inc.**, a Mac repair business in Wilmington's **Trolley Square** neighborhood. Court opinions identify him as the shop owner who recovered data from damaged Apple equipment in April 2019. This repository names **JPMI** for the **copy lineage** attributed to his custody, not as a claim that every later timestamp is his personal act.

Public biographical details that appear in contemporaneous reporting (participant/interview class, not court findings):

- He told [CBS News (14 Oct 2020)](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/hunter-biden-laptop-new-york-post-story/) he is **legally blind** and recognized the drop-off customer by the name given, not by sight.
- [Just the News (25 Jan 2021)](https://justthenews.com/accountability/russia-and-ukraine-scandals/delaware-computer-shop-owner-describes-fbis-first) reported he described himself as not very political, coming from a military family (father and grandfathers).
- [World Tribune (13 Mar 2022)](https://worldtribune.com/john-paul-mac-isaac-faces-ruin-after-going-to-fbi-with-bidens-abandoned-laptop/), quoting the *New York Post*, gave his age as **45** at that time and said he closed the shop after harassment following the October 2020 publication. Local reporting places the close in **November 2020** ([Delaware Business Now](https://www.delawarebusinessnow.com/news/analysis/john-paul-s-mac-shop-closes-for-the-foreseeable-future/article_3a7cc4a7-fd6e-520a-9f0b-566898306935.html)).
- He published *American Injustice: My Battle to Expose the Truth* (2022). Delaware Supreme Court 2025 treats the book as part of the later public record of the dispute, not as filesystem evidence.

Later accounts describe: three damaged machines; staging recoverable data on a **store server**; a customer external drive; preservation copies; sending a copy via his **father** to the Albuquerque FBI; later enlisting his **uncle** to approach Congress.

**See also:** [The Mac Shop](THE_MAC_SHOP.md), [Copy lineages](COPY_LINEAGES.md).

## Brian R. Della Rocca (attorney)

Maryland/DC-area lawyer (Compass Law Partners, Rockville; later Della Rocca Law, Gaithersburg). Delaware bar no. 4447 appears on federal pro hac filings. He is **Mac Isaac's litigation and public-statement counsel**, not the repair-shop technician.

**What he did with the data / the story:**

- Drafted Mac Isaac's October 2020 “clarifying statement” and shopped it to outlets; Just the News published it ([Delaware Supreme Court 2025](https://law.justia.com/cases/delaware/supreme-court/2025/448-2024.html) recites this).
- Told the [Washington Post (30 Mar 2022)](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2022/03/30/hunter-biden-laptop-data-examined/) how Mac Isaac recovered the damaged Mac (boot, copy until crash, repeat) and that Mac Isaac made a copy **before** the 9 Dec 2019 FBI surrender “in case he was ever thrown under the bus.”
- Provided CBS News what he called an **“exact copy”** of the data given to federal investigators — the copy CBS/CFS examined as **untampered, no new files after April 2019** ([CBS, 21 Nov 2022](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/hunter-biden-laptop-data-analysis/)). He called it “clean” because it **predates** widely circulated political clones.
- The JPMI drive arrived via Della Rocca → Todd Sanders ([mailing packet](MAILING_PACKET.md)). Same attorney, same purpose → **byte-identical or virtually byte-identical** with the CBS media; not hash-verified.
- Appeared with Mac Isaac on *Tucker Carlson Tonight* after Hunter Biden's Jan 2023 DOJ letters ([Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/media/hunter-biden-laptop-repairman-responds-legal-threats-presidents-son-gotten-used-it)).
- In Feb 2023, rejected Abbe Lowell's claim that Yaacov Apelbaum was “working with Senator Ron Johnson's office”; Della Rocca wrote Apelbaum **never worked with Johnson** and was asked to **analyze**, not copy ([NY Post, 6 Feb 2023](https://nypost.com/2023/02/06/john-paul-mac-isaac-asks-for-hunter-biden-probe-over-false-laptop-report/)). That is counsel's wording; it sits in tension with Mac Isaac's book (forensic image + Apelbaum retaining a copy — see [Apelbaum](APELBAUM.md)).

He is **not** a forensic examiner of JPMI in this repository's pipeline. He is a **custody/public-record bridge**.

## Computer Forensics Services (CFS) / CBS News

CFS examined the Della Rocca–supplied “exact copy.” CBS reported (21 Nov 2022): no evidence user data was modified, fabricated, or tampered with; **no new files originating after April 2019**; accumulation pattern consistent with normal use stopping around March 2019.

This is **independent forensic review of a Mac Isaac/FBI-lineage copy**. It is not, by itself, proof that CFS hashed the identical Crucial X6 serial `2145E498755E`.
